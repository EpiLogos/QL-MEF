# Q2 — Complete MEF registry and refraction contracts

**Factory coordination:** `EpiLogos/agent-system-design#117`.

Q2 makes the complete twelve-lens MEF manifold executable as deterministic registry data while keeping semantic reading generation outside the registry.

## Stable registry

- 12 canonical `LensRef` identities and 72 local sublenses;
- Square A / Articulation, Square B / Encounter, Square C / Becoming;
- same-face complement relations and settled cross-face Möbius relations;
- deterministic registry lookup and `.0–.5` sublens validation.

The governing L4′ sequence is exactly `Prompts → Traces → Challenges → Patterns → Discovery → Insight`. The older exploratory Observe/Think/Plan/Build/Execute/Verify loop is not encoded as canonical L4′ naming.

## Refraction contracts

`QLTarget` preserves an opaque client-owned subject Ref. `QLProviderRef` and `QLProvenance` record provider/version, operation, result class, inputs/revisions, MEF registry version, optional model and warnings. `QLReading`, `QLRelationReading`, and `QLSynthesis` are derived artifacts that preserve subject identity and provenance; synthesis retains unresolved questions and differences.

Result provenance explicitly separates `canonical`, `deterministic`, `semantic-stochastic`, and `research` classes.

## Deliberate exclusions

Q2 does not generate semantic readings and does not add `locate`, `refract`, `relate`, or `synthesise` provider operations; Q3 owns those operations. It does not encode musical tonics/notes, 8-fold/7-fold derivations, 36/64 state fields, harmonic operators, or speculative role bindings. Those remain research/deeper-operator material unless separately promoted by evidence.
