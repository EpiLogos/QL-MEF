## 13. The T-level loop: NOW thought compressed into #0's self-improvement — 2026-10-30

**Standing:** owner-stated intent and specification, 30 October 2026. It serves the telos track `two-machine-development/self-improvement-pipeline` and the Return leg of #201. Nothing here is runtime, model-effect or human acceptance.

### 13.1 Intent

Close the existing 4/5/0 loop with the machinery already present; build no second agent. During any NOW, the acting body (#0/1) writes short working thought at the twelve T/T′ levels. QL classifies that thought and compresses its sequence. What does not compress — the honest remainder — is the task-log output that Anuttara's #0 self-improvement consumes.

Done when one real NOW, worked by an inhabited agent:

1. produces typed T/T′ thought;
2. has QL read and compress that thought into a T′ distillation carrying bits and unresolved items;
3. has #0 take up the output as a verifier question or a candidate constraint;
4. is followed by a later NOW that visibly uses the result.

Out of scope: graph or canon writes, automatic promotion, model training, new stores or sessions, UI.

### 13.2 The loop, by position and owner

| Step | Position | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | #0/1 | An agent occupies `central:position:control:root:{nara,epii,anuttara}` through `aikit inhabit`. QL tools reach the body through the QL agent skill and `ql epi-agent invoke`. | AIKit, Actuation |

| Compress | QL, at the T level, within the NOW | The sequence of readings is coded under QL's laws: the Day passage T0→T5, the Night passage T0′→T5′ and the conjugate pairing Tn ↔ Tn′ (Discovery with Concealment, Pattern with Anomaly, …), against a plain model of the same sequence. The remainder is the surprising thoughts, the Day readings left without their Night partner, and declared material never consumed. | QL (the `ql-codec` instrument, extended to T sequences) |

Compression is the classifier read the other way: a reading is worth the bits it saves on the material it reads, and what it cannot save is what is new or unresolved. The receipt therefore says what the `compress-thought-artifacts` discipline asks a carry-forward to say — what cohered, what remains open — as a measurement rather than a summary. The QLC-0 fold tax (`ql_core::fold_tax`) is the same quantity at the kernel's own folds.

### 13.5 First measurement — 31 October 2026

**Corpus.** 543 units of agent thought from 40 real Claude Code NOWs (the agent's own narration, in order), and 66 vault thoughts filed under T folders (`Questions` … `Insight`) as a gold set. Nothing in the root `T/` carriers was usable: they held no typed thought.

A second, blind pass never saw the T levels and named each sublens from a shuffled list instead of numbering it. Both passes cost $0.63.

**Measure.** `qlt` (`crates/ql-codec`) codes each labelling as a two-part code, labels then text with the label as context, decodes it again, and compares it with no labels and with the same labels shuffled. Results: `crates/ql-codec/results/qlt-thoughts.tsv` and `qlt-alignment.tsv`.

Findings:

1. **The position is a shared axis.** A thought's T position and the position of its blind MEF reading coincide 28.8% of the time against 19.2% chance (542 thoughts, p < 0.001 over 2000 permutations), and separately within each of L0, L1, L2 and L3: T2 Challenge with How and with Efficient cause, T1 Trace with IS, T5 Insight with Satisfaction/Perishing. L0′, whose sublens names are numerals and would leak position, shows none. Two independently read QL classifications agree on the sixfold position.
2. **The readings are grounded but small.** Every labelling codes the text better than its shuffled self: 230 to 440 bits on the NOW corpus. As a share of what the labels cost, the MEF lens is the most grounded (35%) and the T level the least (13%). No labelling pays for itself as a compression context on 437 KB; class-keyed models split the data too thinly.
3. **Narration has no T sequence.** Within a NOW, the labels cost the same in their real order as shuffled, and the QL cycle Tn → Tn+1 predicts nothing. Test 3 of §13.4 fails on narration. The sequence law has to be tested on thought written at the levels on purpose (test 1), not inferred from running commentary.
4. **The gold folders are filings.** Haiku matches them 35 of 66 times, no better than always answering Discovery, although the relation is above chance (MI p < 0.001).

So in the loop of §13.2, compression earns its place at **Classify**: Epii's check of a declared reading is a grounding measurement, read per lens. It does not yet earn it at **Compress**, as a smaller log, because the structure there was not found.

### 14.3 The reading, turn by turn

1. **Participation.** Epii reads an utterance, the person's or its own, when it chooses to, through its body's Night (P′) reflect tools; nothing requires a reading every turn. Each tool is the native `actuation-research` `reflect.read`: it puts the MEF register's machinery for the lenses it covers to the elected AIKit decision provider (K-E-V) and returns each lens as its profile: every one of the lens's six sub-slots weighed on its own, several of which can hold at once. A square reads each of its positions through that position's L-link pair (register §2). The reading gives:
   - the T positions: the L4′ sub-slots the reading holds;
   - the lens profiles it reads through;
   - once the full text or the three squares are read, the right frame among the lenses (`reflect_right_frame`, `reflect_resonant_frames`).
2. **Whole.** The turn is a whole whose members are its participations, and so is the conversation. The kernel names the grain.
3. **Relation.** Epii declares which earlier participation a new one is set against, composed as row × column. The kernel classifies the pair: family, pair index, direction and D degree. Pairs on the same vertices, such as A1 and C2 on (2,3), stay distinct because the declaration says which.
4. **Frame.** `read` gives the frame pitch, the parts' intervals and their phases (pitch × 30°). `reframe` records any change of Context Frame as a transition.
5. **Recognition (compression).** Recognition happens at two scales:
   - when a 2- or 3-fold is disclosed, Epii may recognise it as one 0/1;
   - when the direct six, the conjugate six and their relational six are all present, it goes 18 → 3 → 1.

   The recognised whole keeps its basis, derivation and operator. `offer_as_whole` makes it addressable.
6. **Carry.** For later turns, Epii's context is the recognised wholes plus the unrecognised remainder, not the transcript. When the talk returns to a whole, Epii reopens it (member focus). The remainder is §13's honest remainder.
7. **Return.** `logos.return` carries the T/C/T′/C′ envelope as it does now.
