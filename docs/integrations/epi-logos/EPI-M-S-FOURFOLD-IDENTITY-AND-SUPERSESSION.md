## 2. Why S/S′ are subordinate reflections of M

`M` names what the subsystem is at the semantic/ontological and guardian register. `S` and `S′` are the differentiated technological ways that identity stands and operates in the present software field.

The dominant/subdominant distinction therefore means:

- `S_i` is the standing product/system body through which the coordinate has a durable technological centre;
- `S_i′` is the operative conjugate through which that same coordinate becomes activity;
- neither reflection replaces the authored Epi meaning of `M_i`;
- neither reflection is an exclusive dependency boundary: a real capability can and often must cross several products and Ta-Onta activities;
- the same-coordinate relation gives constitutional home and stewardship, while cross-coordinate relations give actual composition.

This is why the six current O:I products can be the canonical S layer without claiming that Central *is* Anuttara, Actuation *is* Paramaśiva, and so on as runtime objects. They are typed reflections with distinct native owners.

The embodiment map therefore carries both:

1. the same-coordinate spine; and
