## 0 — What the whole is for

Epi-Logos is to become a working domain world with its own differentiated agency. Nara encounters a person through identity, embodiment, lived history and dialogue. Epii makes that world knowable, teachable and developable. The other M agents supply actual domain intelligence, computations and representations; Ta-Onta gives them the operative world in which to work together.

The owner's latest clarification makes the temporal relation central: **Day/Flow is how Nara comes to know the person over time.** A Day, session, inquiry, creative undertaking or other continuing concern can be read as a journey. One Tarot deck supplies its continuing archetypal vocabulary; I-Ching supplies its change/trajectory modality. The journey develops as the person writes, acts, responds and returns. Nara presents the reading as a native Expression, in the system's own working medium.

This is more than a profile plus a chat, more than an isolated oracle cast, and more than making six agents introduce themselves. The deliverable is a person returning to an intelligible developing world which remembers the relevant history, admits correction, changes through real computation and practice, and helps the next encounter proceed from richer ground.
