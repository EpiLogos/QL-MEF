## 0. Final-pass thesis

The R2 relation becomes much clearer if the comparison is not framed as two software stacks competing for the same functions.

The stronger reading is:

> **O:I develops the objective operative internality of technological/agentic actors and objects. Epi-Logos develops a much denser objective account of human lived internality and its relation to world, meaning, symbolic form, agency and return.**

O:I already uses the term **Objective Internality** for the inspectable operative reality of an actor: projects, histories, sources, capabilities, memories, tools, constraints, environments and other conditions capable of becoming effective in later action. Its own constitution deliberately stops short of claiming a complete model of a phenomenal Subject.

Epi-Logos should not contradict that discipline. Its contribution is not to claim exhaustive access to subjectivity. It is to make selected aspects of human identity, lived condition, activity, symbolic interpretation, personal continuity and reflective change **objectively representable, source-bound, inspectable, revisable and protectable**.

For this document, **Human Objective Internality** is a working name for that Epi concern. It is not yet a new suite-wide canonical term.

The architectural relation is therefore:

```text
O:I Objective Internality
    general operative conditions of human/agent action
    products · context · capabilities · worlds · histories · artifacts · relations
                    │
                    │ human-owned source / operative environment
                    ▼
Epi Human Objective Internality
    structured human identity + condition + activity + meaning + continuity + return
                    │
                    │ selective externalisation only
                    ▼
O:I Objective Co-Internality
    Projection · SharedField · Contribution · Encounter · Other
```

This gives O:I and Epi different but deeply complementary jobs.


This distinction is central to the cross-document matrix.

## 2. Central and Nara — sparse human ground versus integrated human internality

Central and Nara are complementary precisely because Central refuses to become a universal profile system.

### 2.1 What Central intentionally owns

Central defines:

- a human-owned operating root;
- `Control/user`, `Control/agents`, `Control/machines`;
- durable human-authored/adopted source;
- ordinary Work;
- source/observation/inference/derived-state distinctions;
- provenance-bearing change proposals;
- explicit human acceptance before durable Control mutation;
- no fixed schema below `Control/user` unless a specific feature genuinely requires structure.

Central's rule is intentionally sparse:

> natural human self-description is first-class; structured data must earn its maintenance cost.

That is correct general infrastructure.

### 2.2 What Nara intentionally owns

Nara is not a better `Control/user` directory.

Nara is the integrated personal-Pratibimba system in which human identity and lived condition become a structured Epi domain:

- stable identity evidence and identity facets;
- protected personal baseline;
- live transit/condition;
- activity and patterned transformation;
- day/NOW episode and artifact continuity;
- composed personal state;
- symbolic/oracular/cosmic context;
- privacy boundaries;
- review-gated identity augmentation;
- personal/cosmic resonance and lived Epi surfaces.

Its architecture explicitly prevents daily activity from mutating stable identity directly.

### 2.3 The correct binding

The relation should therefore be:

```text
Central human-owned source / personal ground
        │
        │ explicit PersonalGround / IdentitySource bindings
        ▼
Nara integrated identity + lived-state model
        │
        ├─ derived current state
        ├─ protected episodes / activities
        ├─ pattern / meaning observations
        └─ identity-change proposals
                 │
                 │ explicit human recognition / adoption
                 ▼
Central and/or Nara canonical human-owned source updated
```

Central does not acquire Nara's semantic model. Nara does not bypass Central's human-source authority where Central is the source home.

### 2.4 Central source classes and Nara state law are strongly isomorphic

A particularly useful cross-document finding is:

| Central information class | Nara analogue | Authority consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Authored/adopted source | stable identity source / accepted identity facet | may contribute to durable identity |
| Observation | current transit, somatic state, activity event, Encounter evidence | cannot become identity automatically |
| Inference | pattern reading, resonance interpretation, meaning packet | remains derived unless reviewed/adopted |
| Derived state | `Q_composed`, trajectories, computed personal/cosmic relation | rebuildable/subordinate to sources |
| Proposed Control change | identity-augment / internality proposal | provenance-bearing candidate only |
| Explicit human mutation | reviewed/accepted/applied identity update | only legitimate durable promotion path |

This relation should be treated as architectural law, not accidental implementation similarity.


## 4. Foundational Epi primitives that should be lifted above individual M′ implementations

Earlier Epi development often encoded foundational semantics directly in particular services, renderers, folders, packet shapes or UI components. R2 should recover the abstractions before deciding package topology.

The following are the strongest candidates for an explicit Epi foundation layer or shared contracts.

### 4.1 `EpiAddress`

A stable address into the M/Bimba field with:

- canonical coordinate;
- parent/child relation;
- optional legacy `#` alias;
- source/revision;
- QL position/conjugate metadata where applicable.

This must not be equated with a Neo4j node id, URL, UI route or provider key.

### 4.2 `DomainInstrument`

The recurring M′ pattern should become explicit:

> a `DomainInstrument` is the lived operational Pratibimba of one Epi semantic domain, through which deterministic/domain state becomes human- and agent-operable as Actions, Surfaces, state, explanation, sound, image, traversal or conversation.

All six M′ domains are instruments in this sense, even when their experiences differ radically.

A DomainInstrument is not a React component or one AIKit Surface. It may compose multiple Surfaces and provider bodies while retaining one semantic domain identity.

### 4.3 `MathemeProfile` / shared Epi state contract

The existing `MathemeHarmonicProfile` is already functioning as a cross-domain source of tick, harmonic, lens, relation and symbolic state. R2 should treat the shared profile/bus as a foundational contract with explicit writers, readers, revision and readiness rather than a renderer convenience.

This includes the load-bearing Paramasiva → Parashakti → Mahamaya flow:

```text
Prakasa / Paramasiva source state
       ↓ Vimarsha / MEF reading
8 sounded + 4 nodal bus
       ├─ Paramasiva performance
       ├─ Parashakti cymatic/meaning field
       └─ Mahamaya symbolic transcription
```

### 4.4 `PersonalGroundBinding`

An explicit relation from a human-owned ground/source object to Nara without ownership transfer.

Possible sources include Central Control, protected Epi personal source, a user-approved external source, or another future personal-ground provider.

The binding needs at least:

- human/source identity;
- source ref/revision;
- source class;
- authority;
- privacy/disclosure class;
- provenance.

### 4.5 `IdentitySource` and `IdentityModel`

Separate stable identity evidence from its integrated interpretation.

`IdentitySource` is adopted/authored evidence or another explicitly admitted durable input.

`IdentityModel` is a structured synthesis over one or more IdentitySources with provenance. Nara's six identity layers and `Q_identity` are one Epi specialization; the suite must not require every human to use those same identity systems.

### 4.6 `PersonalState`

A current derived state distinct from durable identity. It should be able to preserve, at least conceptually:

```text
stable identity basis
+ baseline/body state
+ external/current condition
+ activity effects
+ composed current state
+ derivation/provenance
```

Nara's quaternion composition is one implementation.

### 4.7 `InternalityObservation`

A provenance-bearing derived account over human/internal state: pattern, resonance, symbolic reading, trajectory, contradiction, correspondence, or another typed interpretation.

It must remain distinguishable from both source fact and adopted identity.

### 4.8 `InternalityProposal`

A proposed change to a durable human/internality model with:

- target;
- proposed delta;
- evidence/observation refs;
- reason;
- privacy/authority basis;
- review state;
- provenance.

It should converge with Central proposal law and Factory Candidate/Recognition patterns rather than create a hidden Nara-only mutation path.

### 4.9 `DomainBridge`

A typed cross-domain relation which preserves:

- source semantic owner;
- target semantic owner;
- operation/reading;
- revision;
- authority;
- provenance;
- whether the relation is read, transduction, proposal, projection or mutation-capable.

This is especially important for M1↔M2, M2→M3, M3→M4, M4→M5 and Bimba↔AIKit/QL/O:I integrations.

### 4.10 `Readiness` as first-class semantic state

The M′ specs repeatedly distinguish implemented, tested, live, provider-absent, dataset-missing, audio-deferred, review-pending and other states. This should be a shared Epi contract rather than renderer-local badges.

Do not make these suite-global merely because Epi uses them deeply:

- quaternion components or `Q_identity` as universal human identity representation;

- Bimba coordinates as generic object identity;

- M′ DomainInstrument as the definition of all applications;
- protected Nara episodes as Central Control by default;
- Epi Pratibimba as O:I Projection.

The abstraction must be lifted; the domain payload remains where it belongs.

## 6. Five semantic collisions that must be made explicit

### 6.1 Pratibimba ≠ O:I Projection ≠ AIKit Projection

```text
Epi M′ / Pratibimba
    native lived operational expression of a Bimba domain

AIKit Projection
    target-specific representation of an effective resolved capability/context view

O:I Projection
    selective shared representation of a native object for an audience/purpose

O:I WorldPresentation
    authored composition/presentation of an explicitly projected world
```

These are related but not interchangeable.

### 6.2 PersonalField ≠ AIKit Context ≠ SharedField ≠ Workcell world

```text
Nara PersonalField
    structured protected lived/internal human field

AIKit Context
    OperativeWorld + InformationHorizon + CurrentFocus

O:I SharedField
    relational environment among independently grounded Participants

Workcell world
    material operational domain / execution world
```

A Nara surface may consume an AIKit Context, run in a Workcell world and selectively project into a SharedField without any of those becoming Nara identity.

### 6.3 Human identity ≠ Control prose ≠ IdentityModel ≠ Participant

Central can hold human-authored source **about** a person. Nara can build an integrated IdentityModel. O:I SharedField can relate a HumanIdentity to a Participant role. None should silently replace the others.

### 6.4 Encounter ≠ human uptake

O:I correctly defines Encounter as what was objectively presented, not what was believed, remembered or internalised.

Human Objective Internality needs a later seam for observable uptake into lived activity or adopted source.

This suggests a **candidate whole-level relation** for future O:I review:

> `Uptake` — an attributable objective relation showing that an actor incorporated an Encountered object into a later action, retained artifact, Context, proposal or recognised state, without claiming subjective belief or phenomenality.

Do not add this to O:I during R2. Record it as a candidate disclosed by the Epi/Nara comparison.

### 6.5 Recognition ≠ automatic promotion

Factory Recognition, Central accepted mutation and Nara identity adoption all share a return law: a candidate or derived difference becomes durable ground only through the authority that owns that ground.

Epi should reuse that general law rather than hiding identity mutation in an inference pipeline.

## 7. Nara, Co-Internality and Explore

The new O:I Explore/WorldPresentation work makes the eventual boundary especially clean.

Nara can own a rich private Human Objective Internality while O:I exposes only deliberate representations:

```text
Central / Nara native personal world
        │
        │ explicit disclosure + privacy/authority check
        ▼
O:I Projection
        │ optional
        ▼
WorldPresentation
        │
        ▼
Explore / SharedField
        │
        ▼
Encounter by Other
```

A public identity projection may expose a chosen name, authored statement, selected interests, public work, a symbolic/artistic representation or another explicit projection while leaving raw identity facets, birth data, journal bodies, derived personal state and protected interpretations local.

This is exactly why Central's sparse human-owned ground and Nara's dense private internality model should stay separate.

This is a **formal resonance matrix**, not a semantic ownership map. Its value is that it shows why the two architectures feel naturally composable without making them the same architecture.

## 10. Final-pass conclusion

The final R2 pass changes the main question from:

> Which pieces of Epi survive migration to O:I?

into:

> **Which general O:I primitives make Epi's human-internality architecture possible, which foundational Epi abstractions must be made explicit above the individual M′ implementations, and which domain-specific meanings must remain irreducibly Epi?**

The strongest answers are now:

1. O:I supplies the general objective-internal operating field and co-internal relation grammar.
2. Central is the sparse human-owned source/root; Nara is the dense integrated human-internality system.
3. Epi needs an explicit foundation layer between O:I and the six M′ instruments.
4. Nara already proves the most mature form of that foundation through its source/condition/activity/composition/review distinctions.
5. The sixfold roles recur fractally across O:I, Epi and Nara, explaining structural compatibility without implying identity.
6. Encounter-to-Uptake is a newly disclosed whole-level gap worth future O:I consideration.
7. Pratibimba, Context, Field, Identity, Projection and Recognition require explicit namespace/non-identity discipline across the suite.

The next artifact is the cross-document matrix that tests these claims source by source against current O:I, Central, Actuation, AIKit, Factory, Workcell, QL-MEF and Epi specifications.
