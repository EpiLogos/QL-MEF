# Q2 MEF registry

Q2 implements the MEF as a versioned Rust contract surface in the standalone QL-MEF repository.

The stable registry contains 12 lenses and 72 local sublenses. It preserves the three squares, same-index conjugate twins, same-face complements, and cross-face Möbius partners. L4′ is fixture-locked to `Prompts → Traces → Challenges → Patterns → Discovery → Insight`.

Client identity remains client-owned. `ClientRef` is opaque, `QlTarget` carries it unchanged, and refraction adds a lens or sublens reading without renaming the subject. A mismatched sublens is rejected rather than coerced.

Reading contracts distinguish single-target readings, relation readings, and synthesis. Provenance records schema and registry versions, provider/version, operation, input refs and revisions, optional model, warnings, and an explicit result class: canonical, deterministic, semantic-stochastic, or research.

`schemas/q2/contracts.schema.json` exposes the same contract names and discriminants to language-neutral clients.

The stable registry deliberately excludes musical assignments, harmonic research structures, 36/64-state structures, and generated semantic role symmetry. Q2 also introduces no Factory Project/Run/Action identities and no Loop Runtime dependency.
