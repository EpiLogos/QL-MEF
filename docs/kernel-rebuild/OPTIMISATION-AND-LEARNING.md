## 4. Comparison and evidence contract

Use the existing story/Run/attempt/matrix evidence. Each selected comparison points to its original A/B/SP and UX obligations, source/subject, hypothesis, candidate change, unchanged invariants, exact baseline/candidate conditions, finite budget, metric definitions, independent verification, outcome and disposition. Retain failures and assistance. Do not create another universal schema, storage service or testing registry for these fields.

Conditions include relevant source/build/install/running revisions, actually loaded context/Skills, model/variant/provider/harness/engine/quantisation/settings, hardware/load/cache state, input sets and source freshness. Unknown or opaque facts remain unknown. Separate occurrence, receipt, astronomical/domain generation, simulation/audio and presentation time.

Measure whole-task input/output/cache usage and spend only where reported; estimates carry dated pricing basis/currency, local resources are not invented token bills, and human effort is separate. Include failures, retries, independent review, synthesis and learning overhead. Give denominators and uncertainty; no accepted outcomes makes cost-per-accepted-outcome undefined. Use repeated/comparable or interleaved trials appropriate to the decision, not one lucky run. Compare equal-budget outcomes and equal-quality resource use where useful.

Freeze the outcome/fidelity contract before comparing. Working agents see ordinary task/context and normal discovery, not expected answers or hidden verifier material. A new source/Skill/build or intervention makes a new condition. Repair restores an intended operation; learning proposes future practice; admission/Recognition makes it operative. Existing grants may allow bounded automation, but none of these steps authorises its own broader permissions or silently changes human ground.
