## 1. The experience being recovered

A person enters a real, continuing world. In its Epi instrument mode, a dated planetary condition around Earth is received through this particular Nara's constitution, active centres and history. It becomes movement, image, glyph, colour and sound. The person can simply encounter it, write about it, or ask Epii to work with what they have selected. Deeper structure opens within the same event: the carrier, spectral relations, apertures, inscriptions, personal interpretation, actual Bimba source and the agent's work remain connected.

The person can change a lens without losing the inscription; open the clock around its centre and gather it again; hear the source-backed musical condition; follow a glyph or centre into its actual properties and Wiki relations; ask for an explanation and a real permitted demonstration; correct the report; return to the same occasion later. A useful operation can become a recognised named method, while an original performance remains replayable as its own occasion.

The purpose is archetypal self-knowledge, attunement and learning through participation. Meaningful sensation is the foreground, with source and explanation available nearby. The whole wisdom/identity/embodiment/oracle/transformation/context/integration field remains present. Its usefulness is judged through actual human encounter; neither a beautiful field nor a passing computation speaks for that judgement.

The agent inhabits that same world. Its orientation, descriptions, mathematics, language, methods, tools, feedback and memory must let it recognise and operate the objects the person is encountering. It needs enough context for the act, not the entire corpus in every prompt. It also needs explicit means of recovering what was meant and what actually happened without asking the person to repeat this architecture.

## 2. Agent UX: how the agent enters and completes the activity

**Orientation.** A fresh session establishes the real Central root/meta-Project or child Project, actor, selected subject, source revision, NOW and available native body. It reads the relevant original intent and whole-field obligation before choosing an implementation or demonstration. Surface focus supplies a possible subject; only the actual disclosure policy makes its source available to that act. Parent access is not ambient child access.

**Discovery.** Source-authored Skill descriptions and actual tool/Action schemas are the interface. The actor finds an applicable Method, reads its prerequisites and exact current owner contract, and confirms its loading/projection basis. The [practice and bootstrap account](AGENT-PRACTICE-AND-BOOTSTRAP.md) distinguishes source presence, catalogue discovery, current projection, harness loading, supported invocation and demonstrated use. A name or SkillSet membership alone proves none of the later states.

**Action.** The actor retains the same subject and original source/world/identity basis through Resolve, C′ composition, native authority, invocation and returned results. The active original CPF/CT/CP/CF/CFP/CS determines the conduct of the work. All operations/horizons are available through the full Vāk field; typed execution stays with its native owner. A question can remain dialogue; a deliberate authorisation can begin execution; uncertainty can return the act to the person. Musical performance reflects actual work.

**Return.** The report tells the person what changed, on what basis, what remains open and what decision is theirs. It expands into actual source, execution and evidence. The person can correct, refuse, redirect or recognise. T/T′ working material is consumed against those outputs and responses. A named reusable path retains its expansion and applicability. Canonical source or identity changes use their own recognised owner operation.

**Re-entry.** A later actor finds the retained decision, source/registry/contract versions, continuation and consumption/Return references. It distinguishes replay from a later reading, a cancelled action from uncertain effects, and a changed provider/body from a changed semantic actor. It does not silently replay a side effect or reconstruct an absent episode from the current sky.

The agent and human therefore participate in one activity. Final proof must use a fresh agent that discovers the practice and actual permitted operation; an implementer manually supplying every hidden identifier is a bootstrap observation, not native UX success.
