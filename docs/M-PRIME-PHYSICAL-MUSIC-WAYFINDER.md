### 1.3 Classify each physical or sonic role

A resonating body is not the same object as a moving emitter, a collision trigger, an analysis display or a symbolic glyph. An Expression may compose all of them. Each relevant entity/field binding must declare which role is operative and what signal/event it produces or receives.

For a resonator, excitation plus material, geometry and constraints determine evolving state. Audio pickups/acoustic observation and visible displacement derive from that state. For a moving source, trajectory and receiver determine spatial rendering. For a collision system, contact events explicitly trigger or modulate synthesis. For an analyser, measured audio is the input and the display is diagnostic. Visual-only changes remain visual-only; they must not be falsely certified as physical synthesis.

That classification prevents a useful but limited particle sampler or audio-reactive shader from standing in for the full resonating instrument.
