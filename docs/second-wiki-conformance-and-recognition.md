## Reciprocal-development law

External evidence may inform QL-MEF, but does not directly mutate its recognised meta-wiki/projection.

The explicit pipeline is:

```text
ExternalObservation
    ↓ propose
MetaAmendmentCandidate(state = proposed)
    ↓ human/authoritative recognition decision
RecognisedMetaAmendment
    ↓ apply
recognised relation in Meta-Knowledge Graph Projection
```

A proposed candidate cannot be passed to `apply_recognised_amendment`; the function requires the recognised type. This makes the type boundary itself part of the governance firewall.

The QW4 proving observation is:

```text
Research Canvas repeatedly observes that
processual reading requires explicit structural-field context.
```

It proposes:

```text
ql-mef:wiki:node:mef-l3
  --contextualised-by-structural-field-->
ql-mef:wiki:node:relation-family-a
```

Before recognition, there is no Glade → Research cross-wiki route because Glade enters through L3 while Research enters through Family A.

After an explicit recognition decision, the recognised relation enters the disposable projection with:

```text
origin = recognised
origin_ref = decision ref
source provider + target ref retained in amendment audit data
```

and the qualified portal can then route:

```text
Glade stable concept
  → Glade MetaBinding
  → L3 Processual
  → recognised internal meta relation
  → Relation Family A
  → Research MetaBinding
  → Research stable Frame
```

The reverse route is also navigable by an `inverse:` traversal label. That inverse label means *navigation against an incoming edge* only; it does not create a second semantic assertion or imply relation symmetry.

## Proposal, recognition, and rejection

Recognition state is explicit:

```text
proposed
recognised
rejected
```

Repeated observations can therefore improve the evidence behind a proposal without silently upgrading it. A rejected proposal remains an audited decision rather than disappearing into an inferred knowledge graph.

Only `RecognisedMetaAmendment` is projection-applicable. The recognised record preserves:

- candidate and observation refs;
- originating foreign provider and target ref;
- from/to QL-MEF meta refs;
- relation/rationale;
- reviewer and decision refs;
- occurrence count;
- source/evidence provenance.

## Rebuild law

Recognised amendments are durable semantic decision records; the graph projection is not.

The acceptance test serialises a `RecognisedMetaAmendment`, rebuilds the projection from authored QL-MEF wiki + MetaBindings, then reapplies the recognised amendment. The same reciprocal route returns without depending on old projection IDs.

## Provider loss and stale revisions

If the Research provider disappears:

- the MetaBinding survives;
- expected target revision remains inspectable;
- availability becomes unavailable;
- foreign payload is absent even if a resolver accidentally offers stale content;
- no Research object is copied into QL-MEF's canonical object set.

Provider availability is operational state, not mapping identity.

## Non-equivalence and Bimba firewall

The resulting Glade↔Research path is a qualified route through recognised mappings and meta relations. It is not a declaration that the two project objects are semantically equivalent. Portal outputs retain:

```text
qualified_relation = true
semantic_equivalence_asserted = false
```

Nothing in QW4 identifies the QL-MEF Meta-Knowledge Graph Projection with the Epi-Logos Bimba Graph. No Bimba bridge is implemented.
