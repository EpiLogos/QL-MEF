# Form

## Contract metadata

- Semantic ref: `ql:skill:ta-onta-form` (`skill/ql/ta-onta-form`), carried in the set `ql:ta-onta-form` under `ql:ta-onta`.
- Native owners: Central (`central.files.resolve`, `central.template.preview`, `central.template.stamp`); AIKit (`aikit wiki-shape`, `aikit wiki-construct`, `aikit wiki validate`); Quaternal Logic (`ql shape bind`, and the law `ql-foundations` and `ql-law` carry).
- Source: `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-FULL-FIELD-LOCK.md` §1 S1′ Hen, SP10–SP15; `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-VAK-COMPOSITIONAL-EXECUTION-LOCK.md` §8 (the six S′ organs as the life of one act); O-I manual `ProjectCentral/docs/1-vision/dlw/DLW.md` §25.3.
- Used by: any agent acting in the agent world, through `skill/ql/ta-onta-methodology`.

Completion: every thing the act will touch has a known form — its residency,
its identity and kind, its standing and revision, and the template, shape or
relation it keeps — decided before any write.

## The route

1. **Where it lives.** `ctrl action run central.files.resolve` for a file's
   place and owner in the ground; residency is decided before any write, and
   a thing that belongs to another register goes there.
2. **What it is.** Read its standing and revision from its owner. Load
   `ql-foundations` and `ql-law` before spelling or reading any coordinate;
   `vak-coordinate-frame` names an act's C′ layers.
3. **Its template.** A page or record keeps its template:
   `ctrl action run central.template.preview` first, then `central.template.stamp`
   on the person's word.
4. **Its shape.** A constellation or board is shaped through QL's law:
   `aikit wiki-shape declare` and `aikit wiki-shape validate` against the
   pinned shape contract, `ql shape bind` for QL's own shapes, and
   `aikit wiki-construct inspect` for a native Wiki whole. The forms of a
   board (Pair, Threefold, Fourfold and its ground or synthesis, Sixfold and
   its conjugate) are Technē's, manual §17.4.
5. **Its relations.** A typed relation is drawn as a proposal and stands only
   when its owner accepts it; a role is not a relation.
6. **Check before it lands.** `aikit wiki validate` for a Wiki file;
   `aletheia-ql-gate` for a QL claim.

## Hands on

The forms and their identities: each thing's residency, kind, standing,
revision and template or shape. Find takes them, to look for what can work on
them.

## Route conditions

- Creating a form is not graduating it; a new page or shape stays a draft
  until its owner admits it.
- No universal frontmatter is required of a thing to have a form.
- Do not build another wiki or content engine. Factory takes part only in
  commissioned work.
- Each return of a changed source keeps its own decision and rollback.
- Graph writes (`bimba-cypher`) are made only where they are authorised.
