# Return

## Contract metadata

- Semantic ref: `ql:skill:ta-onta-return` (`skill/ql/ta-onta-return`), carried in the set `ql:ta-onta-return` under `ql:ta-onta`.
- Native owners: AIKit (`aikit praxis activity`, `aikit history`, `aikit method prove`); Quaternal Logic (the team space's retrospective pass and the six gates); Software Factory (`factory development observations`); Central (`central.now.thoughts.append`, `central.now.learnings.distill`, `projectcentral.now.return`, `projectcentral.source.return`).
- Source: `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-FULL-FIELD-LOCK.md` §1 S5′ Aletheia, SP50–SP55; `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-VAK-COMPOSITIONAL-EXECUTION-LOCK.md` §8 (the six S′ organs as the life of one act); O-I manual `ProjectCentral/docs/1-vision/dlw/DLW.md` §25.3.
- Used by: any agent acting in the agent world, through `skill/ql/ta-onta-methodology`.

Completion: what happened is said from the act's own evidence and set against
its intent; what was learned is returned through its door; anything that would
change a source or a practice waits for Recognition; and the next act knows
where to pick up.

## The route

1. **What happened.** Read the performance Compose kept, and the evidence its
   owners hold: `aikit praxis activity`, `aikit history recent` and `aikit
   history failures`, `factory development observations` for a run.
2. **The retrospective pass.** In the team space the act comes back through the
   frames from what it made: Anima's voices answer three of the six questions
   and Aletheia's Moirai the other three. It is its own inquiry, not the
   forward pass replayed. `prospective-retrospective-pass`,
   `aletheia-expressive-return` and `thought-distil` carry the steps.
3. **Set it against what was meant.** Compare the result with the intent and
   the success conditions. The gates (`aletheia-ql-gate`, `aletheia-m-gate`,
   `aletheia-s-gate`, `aletheia-m-prime-gate`, `aletheia-rupa-gate`,
   `aletheia-collab-gate`) read each claim; `ql-evidence-report` writes the
   evidence up. The person's gate is answered by the person.
4. **Return through the door.** Thought and learning to the register's NOW:
   `ctrl action run central.now.thoughts.append` and
   `central.now.learnings.distill` (root), `projectcentral.now.return` (a
   project). A changed source as a source return for its owner to accept:
   `projectcentral.source.return`. An Expression's reusable material is
   offered to its own owner as a return, and stands only when that owner
   accepts it.
5. **Practice.** A Method run's proof is kept by `aikit method prove` (after
   `aikit praxis method run`); it is evidence, not promotion. A practice
   candidate is tried again on changed inputs and goes to Approvals as a
   proposal; only Recognition makes it a Method.
   `aletheia-improvement-propose` drafts one.
6. **Seed the next act.** Name the next question or the ground it renews, where
   the next act's Enter will find it.

## Hands on

The T/T′ readings, the kept evidence, the returns made through their doors, any
practice candidate waiting for Recognition, and the next question. The next
act's Enter starts from it.

## Route conditions

- Understanding grants no authority to change anything. Return prepares
  Recognition; it lands and saves nothing itself, and the owner keeps.
- No score, critique, diagnosis or test promotes a practice or a source; and
  repetition alone never makes a Method.
- Receiving something grants no authority over its source.
- A replay never erases a failure: a failed take stays in the record.
- Knowledge travels as a return; the wiki is never edited directly.
