# Return what matters

## Contract metadata

- Native source: EpiLogos/QL-MEF; delivered through AIKit's normal Skill system.
- Works on actually supplied/observed material now; automated native thought consumption remains AW3 work.
- Canonical semantics: `docs/kernel-rebuild/VAK-OIKONOMIA-KNOWLEDGE-RETURN.md` §3 and the assigned UX story.

## Inputs and activation

Use after a meaningful observation/action or when a decision is needed. Receive the actual question, subject and source basis, tool result/output/evidence, remaining uncertainties and any human correction. Do not reconstruct an unseen execution from a previous agent's confident summary.

## Authority

A report describes and proposes; it grants no permission, recognises no source on the person's behalf, and does not silently become a personal activity/identity assertion. Keep protected material in its authorised context.

## Procedure

1. Identify the small difference the person needs to understand: result, basis, what remains open, and any decision that is actually theirs. Omit irrelevant procedure narration and invented counterarguments.
2. Check that every execution/source claim is supported by an actual returned observation. Distinguish authored position, interpretation, current support and observed result. State the exact absence when evidence is unavailable.
3. Use the relevant T/T′ reading where useful: Question/Assumption, Trace/Lacuna, Challenge/Affordance, Pattern/Anomaly, Discovery/Concealment, Insight/Integration. Do not force all twelve headings into a response or expose private step-by-step reasoning.
4. Make sources, method and evidence expandable through actual refs/panels. Keep the main report readable. The human can correct, redirect, refuse or recognise; retain their words and the specific claim they concern.
5. Return the useful record through the actual NOW/source/issue route. Consume it only through available native support and appropriate authority into context, Wiki, evaluation, improved praxis or a justified continuing question. Until that operation exists, report consumption as pending and preserve its target and source; do not create a fake consumed receipt.

## Outputs

A concise account grounded in the selected event, with exact evidence and any needed next decision. A corrected claim links to the prior report. Check self-report against retrieved sources, actual outputs, execution and the person's stated concern. Confidence is not substitute evidence.

## Verification

Compare the report with actual retrieval, output and execution evidence and the human’s stated concern. A source-only account cannot claim native execution, and a native result cannot substitute for the person’s assessment.

## Failure and recovery

Missing evidence produces a bounded unresolved statement. Failure includes fabricated progress, hiding a known blocker, repeated architectural restatement, or deleting unconsumed evidence. Preserve original output and human disagreement; do not overwrite them with the revised interpretation.

## Continuity

Keep the report useful to the next participant through current subject/NOW/source and Return references. Redundant working narration can leave the active context under native retention law after consumption; durable evidence remains with its owner.
