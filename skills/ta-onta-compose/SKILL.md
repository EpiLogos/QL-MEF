# Compose

## Contract metadata

- Semantic ref: `ql:skill:ta-onta-compose` (`skill/ql/ta-onta-compose`), carried in the set `ql:ta-onta-compose` under `ql:ta-onta`.
- Native owners: Quaternal Logic (`ql vak compose`, `ql context-frame`, `ql context-frame team-space`); AIKit (`aikit act`, `aikit gateway`, `aikit work task`, `aikit decide`); Software Factory (`factory workflow`, `factory development commission`); Central (`ctrl action run`).
- Source: `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-FULL-FIELD-LOCK.md` §1 S4′ Anima, SP40–SP45; `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-VAK-COMPOSITIONAL-EXECUTION-LOCK.md` §8 (the six S′ organs as the life of one act); O-I manual `ProjectCentral/docs/1-vision/dlw/DLW.md` §25.3.
- Used by: any agent acting in the agent world, through `skill/ql/ta-onta-methodology`.

Completion: the act is composed as a Vāk block QL has checked — its CPF, CT,
CP, CF, CFP, CS, actors, authority and intended return — and performed through
the owners' own operations, with each step's native ref kept.

## The route

1. **Take the basis.** Enter's context, Form's forms, Find's disclosure and
   Time's dated basis. An act with no grounded world is not composed.
2. **Frame it.** `vak-coordinate-frame` names the six C′ layers;
   `ql context-frame list` and `ql context-frame read` give the frames.
3. **The prospective pass.** In the team space (`ql context-frame team-space`)
   the act goes forward through the frames toward what it will make; Anima
   leads in Expressions, Aletheia in Technē, and both teams stand in every
   frame. `anima-orchestration` and `anima-expressive-composition` carry the
   steps; `vak-evaluate` reads a block, and `prospective-retrospective-pass`
   carries an undertaking through both passes.
4. **Write the block and let QL check it.** `ql vak compose <request.json>`
   validates the composition (its `cs-select` step chooses forward synthesis,
   returning inquiry or both); `ql context-frame team-space check` names the
   heads still open. Where heads are open and the person asks, `aikit decide
   invoke --instrument vak-block` asks the decision model to fill them; with
   nothing open, no model is asked.
5. **Choose the body.** The smallest owner that does the act: a native Action
   (`aikit act invoke`, `ctrl action run`), an agent's turn (`aikit gateway
   send`, `delegate` or `team`; `aikit work task`), or commissioned
   development (`factory development commission-compose`, then `factory
   development commission`). A song is scored and run through Factory:
   `factory workflow score`, `factory workflow check`, `factory workflow
   perform`. A scene edit stays a scene edit; it is not made a Factory Journey.
6. **Perform it and keep the refs.** Each step's receipt, run ref or refusal is
   kept as the owner returned it.

## Hands on

The performance: the checked Vāk block, the native refs of everything done,
and every refusal in its owner's words. Return reads it against the intent.

## Route conditions

- Recording a C′ head is not acceptance, and a label is not orchestration:
  only an owner's operation performs.
- An undertaking (CFP Z) needs its authorisation before it runs.
- The decision model is asked only for heads that are open, by the person's
  act, and its provider is never one of the team's own members.
- Factory takes part only in commissioned development.
- Technē's edits cross into Anima to be performed; reading is not performing.
