## The minimal shape

- **# — the Möbius Image** — the generative parent. Inversion as such, negation of negation, void voiding. # is a source, not a position. Self-Identity as (No)Name (Śiva/Prakāśa/Aham) and Power (Śakti/Vimarśa) — the ultimate Self and ultimate Power are one act: Self identifies It-Self.
- **P family** — six Day positions P0–P5 (Torus, orientable, explicate) and six Night positions P0′–P5′ (Klein, non-orientable, implicate). The ontological unit (Truth → Image) is the P Day series, parent = (No)Name; the power unit (Play → Work) is the P′ Night series, parent = Power.
- **Whole anchor 0/1** — the ground that is also the return. The Klein phase map: Phase A (Day, `?`) P0→P1→P2→P3→P4→P5; TWIST P5→P0′; Phase B (Night, `.`) P0′→P1′→P2′→P3′→P4′→P5′; RETURN P5′→P0 (ground renewed). **Return is through the anchor, never 5→0 directly.**
- **Complementary pairs sum to 5** — P0↔P5, P1↔P4, P2↔P3 (and the prime side alike). Each pair carries both Day/(No)Name and Night/Power registers.
- **Pair families A/B/C** (`ql.shape@1.0.0`): A = (0,1),(2,3),(4,5); B = (1,2),(3,4),(5,0); C = (0,5),(1,4),(2,3).
- **Conjugation degrees D1/D2/D3** completing pairs at cardinality 2/3/4 — the 4×4 (16 addresses) and 6×6 (36 addresses) shapes; the **relational sixfold** pairs same-position direct/conjugate as 6+6′, returning through 0/1.
- **4+2**: P0/P5 and P0′/P5′ implicate; P1–P4 explicate. P4 generates internality (the .0–.5 sub-structure originates at the Lemniscate); L0/L0′ is the hinge (positions as questions / as numbers).

## Atomic nodes — 1 vs 0/1

An atomic node is one of two things:

- **Non-QL: literally 1.** A single thing; no opening; no internal coordinate. Most nodes are this.
- **QL: an 0/1.** A node that is both ground and opening — it opens onto the compression ontology developed around the etymological shapes. An 0/1 node is a disclosed whole: partial shapes are valid disclosed wholes (`partial_shapes_are_valid_disclosed_wholes`).

Neither kind is a gate. Everything in a wiki is ql-shaped as a *result* of being in the wiki, not before — relations between ideas arrive in forms, and the wiki uses QL semantics around structure and relation; it does not pre-validate them.
