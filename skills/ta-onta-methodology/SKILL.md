## What Ta-Onta is

Ta-Onta specifies Epi as an internal agent world: what its objects mean, how a
situation is constituted, which powers can be used, how an act is composed,
what is encountered, and what returns into useful knowledge and praxis. It is
the agency definition, and it carries itself: its SDK/API, the manual, the
agent definitions, these Skills and Methods and the surface contracts express
one specification over the native O:I substrate. It is not an interface, and
it is not a layer beside the products.

The products already own everything an act touches. Ta-Onta adds no registry,
store, workflow engine or harness beside them. These skills are that one
specification written so an agent can carry it: the same names a person and a
program use, reached through the product that owns each one.

This Methodology orients and points. The locks named in the contract metadata
carry the law; where this text and a lock differ, the lock is right and this
text is owed a correction.

## One act, six powers

An act lives through six powers. In the order of one act's life:

| Power | Organ | Method | What it hands on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enter | Khora, S0′ | `ta-onta-enter` | The exact world, subject, source basis and continuation the act stands in |
| Form | Hen, S1′ | `ta-onta-form` | Each thing it works on in its form, with its standing and revision, before any write |
| Find | Pleroma, S2′ | `ta-onta-find` | What is actually available, selected and permitted, each attributable to its owner |
| Time | Chronos, S3′ | `ta-onta-time` | The act's times bound together: currentness, epoch, occasion and run |
| Compose | Anima, S4′ | `ta-onta-compose` | The act composed through full C′ Vāk, checked by QL and performed through the owners' own operations |
| Return | Aletheia, S5′ | `ta-onta-return` | What happened set against what was meant, and the return prepared for Recognition |

The powers take part at once. The order is the life of one act, not a pipeline
and not a sequence of calls: no call is compulsory, and a small act may need
only one power. What must hold is what each hands on: Compose never performs on
a world Enter did not ground, and Return never reads an act whose times Time
did not bind.

The cycle closes into renewed ground. What Return brings back is where the
next act's Enter picks up.

## Choose the power by the act

A power is the same power in every mode; what it meets changes. Ask which
power the act is in now, then load that Method:

- Opening a session, a subject, a scene or a set, or resuming one: Enter.
- Placing, naming, typing or relating a thing, or reading a source's standing:
  Form.
- Looking for knowledge, a skill, a method, a tool, a model or an instrument:
  Find.
- Asking whether a reading is current, which run or occasion it belongs to,
  what the Day and NOW hold (Central's), or what the sky and the clock say
  now: Time.
- Deciding who does what, in what order, toward what return, and doing it:
  Compose.
- Saying what happened, comparing it with the intent, and preparing what was
  learned for Recognition: Return.

## Two passes through the team space

Compose and Return are carried by the two teams, Anima and Aletheia, as a pair.
Anima leads an Expression's act and Aletheia a Technē act; neither acts alone.
An act passes through the team space twice, and a pass is a direction of the
context sequence (CS), never a team:

- The **prospective** pass goes forward through the context frames toward what
  the act will make (QL's `forward-synthesis`), asking the standard
  positions' questions.
- The **retrospective** pass comes back through them from what the act made
  and sets it against what was meant (QL's `returning-inquiry`), asking the
  prime positions' questions, P0′–P5′. It is an inversion with its own
  questions, not the forward pass replayed.

Both teams stand in every frame. In the retrospective pass Anima's voices
answer three of the six questions and Aletheia's Moirai the other three.
`prospective-retrospective-pass` carries an undertaking through both. The
passes are directions of a logical sequence, not clock time: the civil Day and
NOW are Central's, and Time relates them without owning them.

## Every operation through its owner

An agent reaches each operation by the name its owner gives it, through that
owner's own command or Action. It never keeps a second copy of what an owner
holds, never writes around an owner's store, and never treats its own
understanding as authority to change anything.

- Resolving a tool grants no permission. The owner that executes an
  operation decides whether it may run.
- A label is not an act. Recording a C′ head, a gate or a pass is not
  acceptance and not a performance.
- An operation that does not exist yet says so, by the name it would have and
  the owner who would build it. Nothing stands in for it: no fixture, no shadow
  store, no "successful" placeholder.
- A refusal is an answer. Pass it on in the owner's words.

## Where each return goes

Every act ends in a return, and every return has one door. Return prepares
Recognition; it lands and saves nothing itself.

- What the act learned or thought goes to the register's NOW, through
  Central's own Actions; knowledge travels as a return, and the wiki is never
  edited directly.
- What it changed in a source goes back as a source return for the owner to
  accept.
- A practice candidate (a composition that worked) is tried again on changed
  inputs and waits for Recognition. Neither one success nor repetition
  promotes it: only Recognition makes it a named Method, with its expansion,
  applicability, source and evidence still inspectable.
- Evidence and readings stay with the run or the act that produced them.
