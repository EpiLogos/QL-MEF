# Find

## Contract metadata

- Semantic ref: `ql:skill:ta-onta-find` (`skill/ql/ta-onta-find`), carried in the set `ql:ta-onta-find` under `ql:ta-onta`.
- Native owners: AIKit (`aikit search`, `aikit knowledge`, `aikit act`, `aikit praxis`, `aikit models`); Quaternal Logic (`ql vak locate`, `ql capabilities`); Central (`ctrl actions`).
- Source: `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-FULL-FIELD-LOCK.md` §1 S2′ Pleroma, SP20–SP25; `docs/integrations/epi-logos/TA-ONTA-VAK-COMPOSITIONAL-EXECUTION-LOCK.md` §8 (the six S′ organs as the life of one act); O-I manual `ProjectCentral/docs/1-vision/dlw/DLW.md` §25.3.
- Used by: any agent acting in the agent world, through `skill/ql/ta-onta-methodology`.

Completion: the act holds a bounded disclosure of what it can use, each item
attributable to the owner that disclosed it, with its standing (grounded,
partial or empty) said, and nothing claimed that was not disclosed.

## The route

1. **Knowledge first.** For what a project knows or decided, ask the
   knowledge route before any raw file search: `aikit knowledge search`,
   `resolve`, `read`, `relations` and `route`, and `aikit wiki query`. Raw
   search is for code-local lookups, or after the route returned nothing.
   `gnosis-retrieve` and `relational-graph-traverse` carry the QL side.
2. **Practice.** `aikit praxis list --form method` (or `--form methodology`),
   `aikit method list`, `aikit set show <set>`, and `aikit praxis disclose` for
   what this act actually carries.
3. **Operations.** `aikit search` across the composed field, `aikit act
   discover` and `aikit act describe <action>` for what can be done here;
   `ctrl actions` and `ctrl action describe <id>` for Central's Actions.
4. **Instruments.** `ql vak locate` and `ql vak context` for where an act
   stands in Vāk; `ql service negotiate` for an instrument's terms;
   `ql techne reading` for a Technē reading.
5. **Say the standing.** Each find is grounded, partial or empty, and says
   which owner answered and at which revision. Widen the bound only on need.

## Hands on

A bounded, revisioned disclosure, expandable on need: the sources, practices,
operations and instruments the act may use, each attributable. Time binds
their currentness; Compose chooses from them.

## Route conditions

- Skills say when, why and how; the native owners enforce permission and
  execute. A disclosed tool is not a permitted one.
- A stale reading never stands in for current text; say its revision.
- Protected material is never copied into a public context.
- Familiarity is not truth or authority: what was used often is not thereby
  right.
