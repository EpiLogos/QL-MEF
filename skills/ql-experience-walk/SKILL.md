# Walk the lived instrument

## Contract metadata

- Native source owner: EpiLogos/QL-MEF; AIKit owns Method/Skill discovery and projection.
- Canonical scenario source: `docs/kernel-rebuild/UX-SPINE-RECONCILIATION.md` §6.
- Host spine: O:I `docs/cradle/04-VERIFICATION.md` §2/§8 and `03-UX-STATES.md` A–K/L.
- This procedure prepares and records tests. It supplies no new domain Action.

## When

Use when an implementation is ready to demonstrate an intended human activity, or to identify precisely why that activity is blocked. All related source-defined capabilities remain in scope beyond a selected walk.

## Inputs

Preparation output from `ql-experience-prepare`, actual installed versions and source refs, a real authorised subject/World/NOW, designated agent session and loaded practice, supported native operations, and the person's agreement to the test. Disposable subjects are appropriate for controlled engineering branches; their result is not the person's lived assessment.

## Authority

The person controls disclosure, consequential acts and recognition through current native owners. Use reversible/disposable negative cases. Do not alter private Control or identity to make a test pass. Do not simulate a provider or conversation without labelling that reduced test explicitly.

## Procedure

1. Read the selected complete story and its A/B/native dependencies. Check each prerequisite before inviting the person to begin. Record source-only, unavailable or blocked branches rather than substituting a mock.
2. Have a fresh real agent orient, discover the relevant Method/tool, read its description/schema and select the supported native route. Record the actual loading/context and invocation basis. Manually supplying every hidden identifier records a bootstrap gap, not native UX success.
3. Let the person perform the stated entry, selection, writing, question or operation. Observe what they can see, understand, control and stop. Retain native subject, source, generation and authority through field, graph, conversation and pop-outs.
4. Exercise the story's failure, refusal, stale context, correction, cancellation and re-entry conditions through genuine supported boundaries. Preserve attempts, uncertain effects and previous source; never blindly repeat a side effect.
5. Read the actual result from its owner, ask the person for their own assessment, and retain their response distinctly. Report through `ql-evidence-report`. Complete the relevant Return or T/T′ consumption only through an available native operation.
6. Record the exact result per obligation and the next owner work. A single passing branch cannot close the complete capability field. Repository/controlled-host evidence, installed real-provider use and human assessment have separate receipts.

## Outputs

Story/A/B IDs, starting material and versions, actual agent discovery/loading and tool route, human actions, before/after evidence, control/refusal/recovery outcomes, returned source/consumption refs, personal assessment or its explicit absence, and exact blockers. Use the actual Central/NOW/evidence owner; no private journal is copied into public CI.

## Verification

The trace checker proves coverage and references only. A live provider/agent receipt proves only its observed scope. Human-validated requires the person's actual assessment of the real activity. Do not invent numerical measures of beauty, attunement or healing.

## Failure and recovery

When prerequisites are missing, return before the person encounters hidden setup. If the route fails mid-walk, preserve the usable state, record the native error/effects and let the person stop or choose the supported recovery. A negative test passing does not make the positive activity available.

## Continuity

Retain original versus later interpretation, current versus pinned/replayed source, precise unfinished work and the person's decisions. Another participant should resume from those records without asking the person to restate the architecture.
